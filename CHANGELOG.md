# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-04

- Desktop month grid with Gregorian dates and alternate-calendar labels; Chinese is the default.
- Alternate date text at 80% of the day number by default, adjustable from 50% to 120%.
- Selected date and its full alternate date in a footer below the grid.
- Daily rollover that follows today unless another date is selected; **Today** resumes following.
- Panel icon with a pinnable popup, System/Light/Dark themes, desktop opacity, week numbers.
